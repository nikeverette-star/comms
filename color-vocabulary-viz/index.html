We name a whole rainbow, but our words clump

Two views of the xkcd Color Survey — 954 crowd-sourced color names — showing how our vocabulary for color pools around a few favorite hues instead of spreading evenly across the spectrum.

The drip chart

Static hero. Each hue family hangs as a column of paint; length = how many names it holds.

The spiral

Interactive. All 954 names wound outward by hue — hover to read one, filter by family.

Data: xkcd Color Survey by Randall Munroe (xkcd.com/color), CC BY-NC.
